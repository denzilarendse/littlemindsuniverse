LittleMindsUniverse · LittleMinds Connect

Privacy Policy

Effective: 28 September 2026 · Last updated: 28 September 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy describes how LittleMindsUniverse handles personal information in the LittleMindsUniverse learning service and LittleMinds Connect. LittleMindsUniverse is a teacher-led education platform with parent/guardian-managed learner profiles and relationship-authorized school communication.

For a privacy inquiry or an account/data deletion request, use our Privacy & account deletion request page. It works in a standard browser and does not require the Android app to be installed.

Information we handle

Depending on the role and features used, the service may handle:

  • Account information: email address, display name, account role, preferred language and country code.
  • Managed learner information: learner display name, learning stage, curriculum, country, verified guardian relationships and classroom membership.
  • Learning records: assigned work, learner responses and evidence, teacher reviews, mastery evidence, progress and approved reports.
  • Classroom communication: authorized conversation participants, message text, reply relationships, timestamps, delivery/read state and moderation or safety records where applicable.
  • Service and entitlement records: trial, subscription or sponsored-access state and transaction/order references needed to provide access and reconcile payments.
  • Feature-specific content: information a user chooses to submit to Milo or other supported learning tools, and optional evidence files where that feature is enabled and authorized.

LittleMinds Connect does not require users to publish private phone numbers to classroom participants. Learner accounts do not currently have an unrestricted message composer.

Why we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, provide role-appropriate learning, maintain verified family/school relationships, deliver teacher-approved work and reports, calculate evidence-based mastery, provide secure classroom communication, provide purchased or sponsored access, prevent abuse, protect children, investigate service or safety issues, and operate and secure the platform.

Where Milo or another AI-assisted feature is used, the content needed for that request may be sent to the configured AI processing service to generate the requested educational assistance. Users should avoid including unnecessary personal information in AI prompts.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers only as needed to operate platform features. These may include Supabase for authentication, database, storage and realtime services; hosting and delivery providers; configured AI inference providers for Milo; payment providers such as PayFast when payment features are used; and Meta/WhatsApp Business services when an authorized WhatsApp feature is enabled and used.

Role and relationship checks restrict educational and communication data to the users and staff who are authorized for that learner, classroom or organization. We do not expose server secret keys to browser or Android clients.

Children and learner safety

LittleMindsUniverse is designed for children and teenagers as well as the adults who support them. Parent/guardian and teacher relationships are verified before protected learner information or classroom communication is made available. Teacher authority is preserved for academic actions, and learner messaging is intentionally restricted while age-safe communication features are developed and tested.

Security

Production traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. The platform uses authentication, row-level database controls, role- and relationship-based authorization, server-side secrets for privileged operations, and restricted private evidence/message access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but we design the platform to limit access to the minimum required for each role.

Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as it is needed to provide the service, maintain child-safety and relationship integrity, resolve disputes or security incidents, meet accounting/payment obligations, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Users can request deletion of their LittleMindsUniverse account and associated data through the in-app account path or the public Privacy & account deletion request page. A verified request is placed into a controlled deletion process. Data that can be deleted is removed or de-identified as part of that process. Limited records may be retained where necessary for safeguarding, security/fraud prevention, transaction/accounting requirements, dispute handling or another legal obligation; retained records are restricted to those purposes.

Managed learner records are reviewed against verified guardian, teacher and school relationships so that one account cannot erase information another authorized guardian, learner or school is entitled or required to retain. This review does not cancel the deletion request.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can sign out, update a password through the password-recovery flow, and request account/data deletion. You can also submit a privacy inquiry from the public request page after verifying the account. This gives LittleMindsUniverse a working privacy-contact mechanism without publishing an unverified email address.
