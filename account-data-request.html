Application account & privacy requests

This public web resource lets a LittleMindsUniverse account holder ask us to remove their application account and associated data, or submit another privacy inquiry, without installing the Android app.

Application account removal

A verified removal request starts a controlled process for the signed-in LittleMindsUniverse application account and associated data. It is not a temporary account freeze. Data that can be removed is deleted or de-identified as part of fulfillment.

Limited records may be retained only where necessary for child safeguarding, security or fraud prevention, transaction/accounting requirements, dispute handling, or another legal obligation. Linked learner and classroom records are reviewed so one account cannot erase records another authorized guardian, learner, teacher, or school is entitled or required to retain. See the Privacy Policy.

What happens next

After a verified request is submitted, LittleMindsUniverse records it for controlled processing. If identity, child-safety, school-relationship, or legal-retention questions must be resolved, the request can be reviewed before final fulfillment. The request remains active while that review occurs.
