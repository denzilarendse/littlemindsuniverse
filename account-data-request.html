Delete your LittleMindsUniverse account

This public web resource lets a LittleMindsUniverse account holder request deletion of their app account and associated data without installing the Android app. You can also use it to submit another privacy inquiry.

Account and associated-data deletion

A verified deletion request starts a controlled process for the signed-in LittleMindsUniverse app account and associated data. It is not a temporary account freeze. Data that can be deleted is removed or de-identified as part of fulfillment.

Limited records may be retained only where necessary for child safeguarding, security or fraud prevention, transaction/accounting requirements, dispute handling, or another legal obligation. Linked learner and classroom records are reviewed so one account cannot erase records another authorized guardian, learner, teacher, or school is entitled or required to retain. See the Privacy Policy.

What happens next

After a verified deletion request is submitted, LittleMindsUniverse records it for controlled processing. If identity, child-safety, school-relationship, or legal-retention questions must be resolved, the request can be reviewed before final fulfillment. The deletion request remains active while that review occurs.
